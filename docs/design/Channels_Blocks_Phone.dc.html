Channels

A channel plays one theme on a set of speakers. Each card is one channel.

Each channel

Theme: the sound the channel plays. Picking another theme also picks that theme's default preset, if it has one.

Preset: a saved mix of the theme's tracks. Default settings plays the theme without a preset.

Sequences: listed in the Preset menu after the presets, marked with the sequence icon. Picking one plays its presets in turn; the line under the field shows the preset playing now and when it changes. Picking a preset stops it.

Playlist: a channel can play a playlist instead of one theme: in Edit, choose Playlist. The card then shows the playlist and the entry playing now.

Next: plays the playlist's next entry now.

Speakers: where the channel plays. Use Edit to change them.

Volume: the channel's volume. It is saved when you let go of the slider.

Play / Pause: starts or stops the channel on its speakers.

Edit: changes the channel's name, theme or playlist, preset, volume and speakers.

Delete: removes the channel. You confirm first.

New channel

New Channel: makes a channel. Leave the name empty and Sonorium names it after the theme and speakers.

Speakers: pick floors, areas or single speakers. Only speakers turned on under Settings › Speakers are listed.

Speaker group: picks a saved group instead of single speakers. Shown when you have speaker groups.

Playing count: the number next to Channels in the menu is how many channels are playing.
