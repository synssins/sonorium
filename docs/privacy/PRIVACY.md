# Sonorium Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026

**Sonorium doesn't collect your data. The app has no analytics, no ads, no crash-reporting service, no developer accounts and no developer servers. Everything the app stores stays on your phone, and the app talks only to your own Sonorium server and the speakers on your home network. The one exception is a Pro purchase: Google handles the payment and passes the developer a limited order record, used only for refunds, support and tax records.**

This policy covers the Sonorium app for Android, made by Synssins, and the sonorium.app website.

## What the app stores

The app keeps these on your phone, in storage only the app can read:

- the addresses of the Sonorium servers you link it to;
- the key each server gave the app when you linked it;
- your settings;
- themes you download (Pro);
- whether you bought Pro.

None of it is sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android's settings, deletes all of it.

## Who the app talks to

- **Your Sonorium servers.** You choose them, by scanning the code your server shows or by typing an address. They're normally on your home network. Your server is yours: what it stores, such as its user accounts, stays on it and is outside this policy.
- **Speakers on your home network** (Google Cast, DLNA, Sonos, HEOS, AirPlay), found by asking the network which devices are there.

The developer never sees any of this traffic.

## The link code

Sonorium's Link App code is a web address on sonorium.app with your server's details after a `#`. With the app installed, Android opens the app and the details go straight to it. Without the app, the address opens a page on sonorium.app. The part after the `#` stays on your phone: browsers don't send it to websites.

## The sonorium.app website

sonorium.app is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no cookies, analytics or ads. GitHub keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Its pages load their font from Google Fonts, so Google receives your browser's IP address when you open them, under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). This privacy page loads nothing from other sites.

## Pro purchases

Google Play handles the payment under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Through the Play Console, Google gives the developer a limited order record: the order number, date and product, and possibly the buyer's name, email address and approximate location. The developer uses it only for refunds, support and legal and tax records, never for marketing.

## Google services the app uses

- **Google Play services and Google Cast**, to cast to Cast speakers.
- **Android vitals.** If you've allowed it in your phone's settings, Google collects crash and performance data about apps, including this one. That's Google's collection, under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); the developer sees only summaries without your identity.

## Permissions

- **Network and Wi-Fi access:** to reach your Sonorium server and speakers, and to find them on your network.
- **Nearby devices** (on newer Android versions): to find speakers on your network. The app doesn't use your location.
- **Notifications:** for the playback controls.
- **Background playback:** so audio keeps playing with the screen off.
- **Camera:** only when you scan a Link App code. The picture is read on the phone and isn't stored or sent anywhere.
- **Fingerprint, face or screen lock:** when you ask the app for a sign-in code, it checks your phone's own lock first, if your phone has one. The app never sees your fingerprint or face; Android only tells it whether the check passed.

The app doesn't ask for your location, contacts, microphone or files.

## Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and the developer doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Sharing and selling

The developer doesn't share or sell data, uses no data brokers and shows no ads.

## Your rights

The only personal data the developer may hold is Google's order record for a Pro purchase. To see it or have it deleted, email sonorium.app@gmail.com. Data in your Google account, including your purchases, is managed by Google at [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com).

## Security

The app's data stays in its private storage on your phone. The app controls your server over an encrypted connection and checks that the server is the one you linked. The audio itself comes from your server over plain HTTP on your home network, the same way speakers receive it.
